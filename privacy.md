# Trophe Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Trophe is operated by Nicolo Luca Bernardini, Via Sant'Antonio 3, Locarno, Switzerland, who is responsible for your data (the "controller"). Contact: olocin27@gmail.com.

Trophe has no establishment and no representative in the European Union or the United Kingdom. Wherever you live, you can write directly to the controller at the postal or email address above, in English or Italian.

## Data stored on your device

Trophe stores the following on your iPhone: your name, goal, diet, allergies, personal details (such as age, sex, height and weight), your answers to the starting check (such as supplements, periods and tiredness), the supplements you take and tick off, meals, water, activity, and reminders. The starting check answers and your supplements never leave your device. This data stays on your device unless a section below says otherwise. You can remove it with Settings > Start over, or by deleting the app.

## Meal photos and voice input

If you agree in the app, each meal photo you take or choose, the text of a meal you dictate, and the ingredient list of a recipe you import, passes through our server, hosted by Supabase, Inc., and is sent to Anthropic, PBC, an AI company, to recognize the food. The list of foods in the app goes with it, including the names of products you scanned. Nothing is sent without your agreement, which the app asks for the first time you use a photo, voice or recipe import. To withdraw it, write to the contact address above.

Anthropic processes this data on our behalf. It does not use it to train its models and deletes it within 30 days, except content flagged for a breach of its usage policy, which it can keep for up to 2 years (https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data). Trophe does not save your photos or dictated text on its server.

Voice input uses Apple's speech recognition: your audio is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and on your device only if Apple's service is not reachable.

To limit how many photos and dictated meals can be analyzed, the app creates an anonymous account with Supabase, without your email or name. It is separate from the optional account below. The app also uses Apple's App Attest, which lets the server check that requests come from the real Trophe app on a real iPhone: it creates a key on your iPhone, and the server keeps that key's public part. The server stores scrambled (hashed) versions of this key's ID and of your IP address, with a daily count, and deletes them after about a week. The anonymous account ID is also your app user ID with RevenueCat, which the server uses only to check whether you have Trophe Pro. The anonymous account is deleted after 30 days without use, or when you delete your account.

## Recipe links

When you import a recipe from a link, the app opens that page directly from your device to read the recipe, as a browser would: the website receives your device's IP address. No personal details are sent with the request.

## Barcodes

When you scan or type a barcode, the code is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product. No personal details are sent with it.

## Food pictures

Pictures of foods load from Wikimedia Commons, which receives your device's IP address when they load, as with any web page. No personal details are sent with the request.

## Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Trophe reads your weight, steps and workouts to adjust your daily goals. Trophe never writes to Apple Health. Steps stay on your device. Weight and workouts are stored with your other data and go to your account only if you create one (see below). Health data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

## Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. Trophe uses RevenueCat, Inc. to check your subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. Trophe does not receive your payment details.

## Accounts and sync

Accounts are optional. Before you create one, the app asks for your consent. With an account, your email address and your Trophe data are stored with Supabase, Inc. so they sync between your devices: your name, personal details, goal, diet, allergies, weight and weight history, meals, water, workouts (also from Apple Health), recipes, scanned foods, favorites and reminders. Your starting check answers, your supplements and your profile photo are never uploaded.

You can sign in with email, with Apple or with Google. Apple or Google then shares your email address with us, and your name if you allow it. Apple can hide your real email address. If you sign in with Apple, the server keeps a token from Apple, used only to revoke Trophe's access to your Apple ID when you delete the account.

You can delete your account at any time in Profile > Account and sync > Delete account. This deletes your synced data, your feedback messages sent while signed in, the Apple token and the anonymous account used for food recognition, and revokes the Apple sign-in. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, which you manage in your Apple settings.

## Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, we store your message with the app version, iOS version and language. If you are signed in, it is linked to your account and deleted with it. Feedback sent without an account is kept for 2 years and is not linked to you.

## Usage data

Usage data is off unless you turn it on in Settings > Usage data. It helps us learn which parts of the app help and where people stop. It uses PostHog, Inc., with servers in the United States. When it is on, the app sends:

- events such as "intro completed", "check completed", "meal logged", "app installed", "app opened" and "purchase completed", the last one with the plan and whether it started a free trial;
- a random ID created by the app, not linked to your name, email or account;
- device details: device model and manufacturer, iOS version, app version, language, time zone and screen size.

This data is pseudonymous: it is not linked to your name, but the random ID connects your events. It never includes your meals, foods, health answers, name or email. Your location is not looked up from your IP address, and PostHog discards the IP address. PostHog keeps the events for one year.

While usage data is on, RevenueCat also sends PostHog the subscription events it receives from Apple, such as a trial that becomes paid, a renewal or a cancellation, with the same random ID. When you turn it off, the app stops sending events and removes the random ID from RevenueCat. Start over gives the app a new random ID.

## Why we use your data (legal bases)

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland:

- meal photos and dictated meals sent for recognition: your consent;
- account and sync: to provide the account you asked for, and your explicit consent for health data such as weight, workouts and diet;
- subscriptions: to provide the subscription you bought;
- usage data: your consent;
- photo and dictation limits, and feedback: our legitimate interest in keeping the service working and affordable, and in improving it.

You can withdraw a consent at any time by writing to the contact address above, for usage data in Settings > Usage data, or for the account by deleting it. Withdrawing does not affect what was processed before.

## Where your data goes

Anthropic, RevenueCat and PostHog are based in the United States, and PostHog stores usage data there. Supabase stores our data in the European Union (Stockholm, Sweden). Transfers outside your country are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, which are part of each provider's data processing agreement.

## How long we keep it

- Data on your device: until you use Start over or delete the app.
- Account data and the Apple token: until you delete your account.
- Photo and dictation counts: about a week.
- Anonymous account for food recognition: 30 days after its last use, or until you delete your account.
- App Attest public key: 90 days after its last use.
- Feedback send counts (scrambled account ID and IP address), used to stop spam: one day.
- Usage data: one year.
- Meal photos and dictated text at Anthropic: up to 30 days, as described above.

## Tracking and advertising

Trophe does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, shows no ads, and does not sell your data.

## Children

Trophe is not directed at children under 13.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, ask us to restrict or stop processing, and receive your data in a portable format. You can export all your app data yourself in Settings > Backup and export. To exercise your rights, write to olocin27@gmail.com. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new date.
