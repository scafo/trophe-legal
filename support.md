# Trophe Support

For questions, problems or feedback, write to olocin27@gmail.com.

## Common questions

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Trophe.

**How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?**
Open the Trophe Pro screen in the app and tap Restore.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app, go to Profile > Account and sync > Delete account. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription.

**How do I export my data?**
In the app, go to Settings > Backup and export.
